My own AI assistant.

It reads Excel models, researches topics and summarizes the news. It can only do what I allow.

My contributionI decided what JARVIS should do and what it may never do, directed AI coding agents to build it, and tested the result.

WHAT IT DOES

Everyday work, done faster.

I ask a question and JARVIS hands it to the right helper: Excel, research, news or calendar. It runs on my own laptop.

THE RULE

Nothing happens without permission.

Before JARVIS touches a file or a tool, one central check decides whether that is allowed. A stop switch halts everything.

HOW IT COULD HELP YOU

For a finance team.

It checks a budget model before a meeting and lists the exact cells someone should review. People still make the decisions.
