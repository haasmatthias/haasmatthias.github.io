ATREMETA / FINANCIAL MODEL

A full financial model for a capital raise.

Atremeta recovers silver and other value metals from old solar panels and zinc-production residues. For its capital raise, I built one connected Excel model that shows how money enters the business, what it pays for and what is left for lenders and investors.

FOLLOW THE MONEY
Money in↓Costs + loans↓What remains?
One connected picture.

My contributionI designed every block: the structure, the debt logic, the cash lock-up and the tax treatment. AI coding agents sped up the build; I reviewed and tested every part and can walk you through any formula.

WHAT IT DOES

One plan, fully connected.

Project companies, a holding, bank debt with DSCR covenants (tests whether cash flow covers the loan payments), taxes, a dividend waterfall and investor returns, across four scenarios. Change one assumption and every result updates.

THE RESULT

Ready for due diligence.

Built in under four months, the model replaced an external modeling advisor and went through institutional due diligence. Automated checks make sure the numbers agree, and every figure traces back to its source.

HOW IT COULD HELP YOU

For a finance team.

I can build and check the models behind financing, budget and investment decisions, and explain them so others can follow the numbers. Confidential figures, locations and partner names are not shown.
