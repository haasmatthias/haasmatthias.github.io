INVESTOR FINDER / PROTOTYPE

Finds investors who actually fit.

A research tool that matches investors to one specific funding round and shows, for each candidate, the evidence, the source and what is still unknown.

LOOK PAST THE NAME
Potential investor

Why might they fit?

What is the source?

What is still unknown?

Evidence before outreach.

My contributionI specified the research criteria, evidence requirements and approval steps, then directed and reviewed the implementation.

WHAT IT DOES

From a list of names to a shortlist.

It compares each candidate with the round: ticket size, mandate and stage. It shows reasons for and against, and possible introductions through known contacts.

THE RULE

No source, no candidate.

AI may not invent people, e-mail addresses or relationships. Every fact carries its source. The tool sends no outreach.

HOW IT COULD HELP YOU

For fundraising, M&A or partnerships.

Faster, traceable screening of investors, targets or partners: a shortlist you can defend. Prototype presented in October 2026.
